01 — THE PROBLEM

Event details arrive everywhere except your calendar.

Invites live in screenshots. Deadlines hide in group chats. Flyers get photographed once and forgotten. Re-typing them is the tax everyone pays — and things still slip.

Fragmented

Input is unstructured

Images, PDFs, voice notes, and messy text — no consistent format to parse.

Manual

Re-typing wastes hours

Copying details into a calendar is slow, error-prone, and easy to forget.

Incomplete

Tools stop at the text layer

Notes apps capture; they don't extract dates, times, and locations into something actionable.

02 — THE SOLUTION

Capture in. Calendar out.

One input box. Under the hood, AI does the reading, structuring, and syncing — so the user does nothing but drop the thing.

1

Drop anything

Images, screenshots, flyers, or pasted text — up to three images at once.

2

AI extracts events

Title, start, end, location, notes, and a confidence score for every event.

3

Syncs to Google

One-click Calendar connection. Upcoming events push automatically; past ones are skipped.
