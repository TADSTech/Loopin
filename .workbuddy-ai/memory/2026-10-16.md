## Phase 2 — 404 page, warning modal, IP banning
- `lib/rate-limit.ts` upgraded: `checkRequest()` records a strike per over-limit call (keyed on client IP) and escalates to an **IP ban** after `RATE_STRIKE_THRESHOLD` (default 8) strikes within `RATE_STRIKE_WINDOW_MS` (10 min); ban lasts `RATE_BAN_DURATION_MS` (15 min). All env-tunable. `getBanStatus`/`clearBan` exposed.
- `lib/ip.ts` `clientIp()` (x-forwarded-for first hop → x-real-ip). Wired into `extract` + `checkout` routes (checkRequest, strikeKey `ip:<ip>`) and the `ingestEvents` action (soft 30/min; returns `abuse` state).
- `proxy.ts` middleware enforces bans app-wide: banned IP → 403 "Access temporarily blocked" page (stops reload/endpoint-switch bypass); otherwise `updateSession`.
- `app/components/warning-modal.tsx` (new) + `page.tsx` derivation → shows rate_limited/banned dialog; dismiss is per-result (ref compare, no effect setState).
- `app/not-found.tsx` (new) custom 404; `AppState.abuse` added in `lib/event-types.ts`.
